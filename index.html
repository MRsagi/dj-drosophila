how training works · honest edition

Hey, I trained a fruit fly to DJ

That’s the gag — and the product. The shared live club is its set: 24/7, same stream for everyone, mind deciding when to leave a track and how long to crossfade. Under the hood this is scientific comedy: MaleCNS is a wiring diagram (CC BY). We use a few named actuator proxies plus a separate DJ mind / unsupervised policy — not a conscious animal, not an upload, not plasticity in the connectome.

Gag

“I trained a fruit fly to DJ — this is its set.”

Truth

Three LIF toys + a canvas + a server-side policy mind with a decision log. DN / GF labels are proxies; the mind scores energy arcs, novelty, and phrase gates. Unsupervised bits learn from mix features — not from a fly’s soul.

What actually runs

  1. Sensors — song visualizer → approx R1–R6 / R8 eye columns (not ommatidia census).
  2. Actuators (MaleCNS-named proxies) — DN-L / DN-R / Giant Fiber LIF stubs lean the xfader, ride master, propose escapes. Proxies ≠ identified cells unless you verify in neuPrint.
  3. DJ mind (live radio) — chooses when to leave and how long to blend (≈8–32s); appends decisions to a show log so every listener shares the same set. Crossfader stays glued to an edge while PLAYING.
  4. Unsupervised policy (lab) — feature streams → clusters, novelty, A/B energy map. Bundled pretrained model auto-loads.
  5. Crate — CC0 OpenGameArt mp3s in public/crate/; procedural beds as fallback. Spotify is out.

Science fine print

  • No consciousness. A LIF stub is not a mind — the “fly DJ” is comedy + engineering.
  • No upload. We did not instantiate ~166k neurons.
  • “Trained a fruit fly” means MaleCNS-named actuators + our policy mind / unsupervised head — not animal training.
  • No artist cloning. Style lanes are genre heuristics.
  • Do not cut flies for a nightclub. This is software.
