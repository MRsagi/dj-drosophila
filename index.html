pipeline

I trained a fruit fly to DJ

One radio. One timeline. The mind on the server picks leave time and fade length. MaleCNS names the toys. The toys are not the cells.

  1. Eyes — spectrum into a lattice. R1–R6 / R8 as labels, not a census.
  2. DNs / GF — three LIF units. Lean, vigor, jump. Check neuPrint before quoting types.
  3. Leave mind — when to cut, how long to blend. Logged so every listener shares the same set.
  4. Booth — filter, echo, loop, pitch on your copy. Encoder stays dry.
  5. Crate — CC0 from OpenGameArt. Footer lists the records.
