# Licenses — DJ Drosophila music crate

Public club audio ships as **CC0** (public domain dedication) tracks from
[OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org), plus procedural CC0-style Web Audio
beds generated in-browser. **Spotify / commercial streaming is not used.**

Attribution is appreciated even when not required. The Club page footer lists
active crate lines from `src/crate/manifest.json`.

## Procedural beds (fallback)

Generated at runtime by `src/crate/proceduralCrate.js` (Ommatidium Pulse, Lattice
Techno, R8 Soft Glow, and related IDs). Treated as **CC0** for this demo — no
external audio files required if the mp3s are absent.

## Connectome data (not music)

MaleCNS connectome data from FlyEM / HHMI Janelia, Cambridge Connectomics Group /
MRC LMB, and Google Research — **CC BY** — dataset `male-cns:v1.0` at
[neuprint.janelia.org](https://neuprint.janelia.org).
